# Stakeholder Review and Approval

## Review record

For each stakeholder or authority, capture:

- role and decision right;
- consulted/approver/informed classification;
- reviewed PRD version;
- decision: approve, approve with constraints, object, or no response;
- requirement IDs affected;
- evidence date and source;
- accepted trade-offs and unresolved items.

## Approval rules

- Product-owner approval is mandatory.
- Legal, security, finance, operations, data, or compliance approval is mandatory only when their decision rights are triggered by scope.
- Do not convert consultation into approval.
- Do not imply approval from silence.
- User approval in chat is valid when the user is the authorized product owner; record that basis.
- A non-blocking absent stakeholder may be tracked as a constraint only when project governance allows it.

## Contradiction handling

When stakeholders conflict:

1. identify the conflicting requirement IDs;
2. state each position and evidence;
3. quantify impact where possible;
4. route to the product owner or named decision authority;
5. record the final trade-off;
6. update the PRD version and review record.
